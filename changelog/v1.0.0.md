## First release

### Classes
- Create a class and share it with a link or a 6-character code
- Students tap the link or type the code, see who invited them, and join
- Classroom-style home: a card per class with header artwork, section and teacher
- Class screen with **Attendance** and **People** tabs; teachers can approve new phones and remove students
- Edit a class's name, section, room and artwork; delete a class (students are told why they lost access)

### Attendance
- **Take attendance** opens a 5-minute session; students who open the app are asked to mark it
- Attendance is proven over Bluetooth with keys kept in each phone's secure hardware, so it can't be marked for someone who isn't in the room
- The teacher sees who is present as they check in; records sync to the class even after being offline

### App
- Google sign-in with your Google photo as your avatar
- Settings: teacher or student, light / dark theme, Bluetooth and permission status
- In-app updates and this changelog
- Works with large text, TalkBack and "Remove animations"
