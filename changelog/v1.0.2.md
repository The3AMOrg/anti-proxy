## Fixes and improvements

### Attendance
- **Each class stays separate.** When two classes take attendance at the same time in rooms side by side, students only connect to their own class's teacher. Before, a phone could pick up the teacher next door.
- A teacher's phone only accepts students of the class whose attendance is running. A student from another of that teacher's classes is told they're not on this class's list, instead of being shown "marked" when they weren't.
- A student's "Your attendance" list shows only that class's check-ins, even when one teacher teaches them several subjects. Check-ins saved before this update can still appear under each of that teacher's classes.
- Check-in reaches further: the distance limit now covers the back of a 10–15 m room, including through a wall or two.

### Classes
- **Get a new code** works again for a class whose old code had already been removed.

**Note:** teachers and students both need 1.0.2. A student on 1.0.2 can't find a teacher still on 1.0.1.
