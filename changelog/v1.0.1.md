## Fixes and improvements

### Attendance
- Attendance now **ends by itself** when its 5 minutes run out; before, the session stayed open until someone tapped End
- Check-in works from across a classroom: a bug stopped students more than about a metre away from being marked, and the distance limit now reaches the back of a 10–15 m room
- Teachers see **who has checked in**, live: the class card lists names and the count, and tapping it opens everyone in the class, with filters for All, Present and Absent
- If Bluetooth is off, Take attendance and Check in ask to turn it on, and carry on once it's on

### Setup
- A one-time "Allow nearby access" step after choosing a role asks for Nearby devices and Location up front

**Note:** this version changes the attendance proof format, so students and teachers need to both update. Phones still on 1.0.0 can't check in against 1.0.1.
